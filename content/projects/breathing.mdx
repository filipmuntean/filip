I built [JustBreathe.baby](https://justbreathe.baby) because I often have anxious moments and wanted a simple breathing timer that works instantly, without the overhead of most meditation apps.

## What it does

- Breathing timer with the 4-4-4 method by default (inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 4s), fully customisable.
- Distraction-free: no paywalls or upsells.

## Stack

- **Next.js**, server-rendered for speed and SEO.
- **Google OAuth 2.0** sign-in.
- **Magic links via Resend** for passwordless login.
- **MongoDB** for user sessions and preferences.
- **Google AdSense** for monetisation.
- **Stripe** payments planned for premium features.

## Planned

- Custom breathing patterns for different anxiety levels.
- Premium techniques via Stripe.
- Tracking features for personal insights.
