## 🌬️ InhaleExhale: Your Guided Breathing Companion

Modern life is stressful, and anxiety can often feel overwhelming. **InhaleExhale** is a simple yet effective web app designed to guide you through breathing exercises that help you regain control and find moments of calm.

### 🧘 Features

✅ **Interactive Breathing Guide** – Follow a visually soothing guide that helps regulate your breathing rhythm.  
✅ **Customizable Sessions** – Adjust the inhale, hold, and exhale durations to match your comfort level.  
✅ **Minimalist UI** – A distraction-free experience to keep you focused on your breathing.  
✅ **Dark Mode Support** – Relax your eyes with an elegant dark mode.  
✅ **Mobile-Friendly** – Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile devices.
